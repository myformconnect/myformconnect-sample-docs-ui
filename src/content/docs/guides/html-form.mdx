Connect any static HTML form to MyFormCapture in under two minutes. You don't need PHP, Node.js, or any server-side logic — simply point your form's `action` attribute to your unique MyFormCapture endpoint.

---

## What you'll build

In this guide, you will create a standard contact form with three inputs:
1. **Name** (`<input type="text" name="name" />`)
2. **Email address** (`<input type="email" name="email" />`)
3. **Message** (`<textarea name="message"></textarea>`)

When visitors submit this form on your website, MyFormCapture captures the submission, runs spam filtering, sends you an email notification, and forwards the data to any configured webhooks.

## Complete HTML form example

Copy and paste the code snippet below directly into your `index.html` file:

<CodeExample
  language="html"
  filename="index.html"
  code={`<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="UTF-8">
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
  <title>Contact Us — MyFormCapture Demo</title>
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css">
</head>
<body>
  <main class="container">
    <h1>Contact Our Team</h1>
    <p>Fill out the form below and we will get back to you shortly.</p>

    <!-- 1. Replace 'demo-form-uuid' with your real form ID from your dashboard -->
    <!-- 2. Keep method set to POST -->
    <form action="https://myformcapture.com/f/demo-form-uuid" method="POST">
      
      <!-- Name Field -->
      <div class="form-group">
        <label for="name">Your Name</label>
        <input 
          type="text" 
          id="name" 
          name="name" 
          placeholder="Jane Doe" 
          required 
        />
      </div>

      <!-- Email Field -->
      <div class="form-group">
        <label for="email">Email Address</label>
        <input 
          type="email" 
          id="email" 
          name="email" 
          placeholder="jane@example.com" 
          required 
        />
      </div>

      <!-- Message Field -->
      <div class="form-group">
        <label for="message">Message</label>
        <textarea 
          id="message" 
          name="message" 
          rows="5" 
          placeholder="How can we help your team?" 
          required
        ></textarea>
      </div>

      <!-- Optional: Custom redirect URL upon successful submission -->
      <input type="hidden" name="_next" value="https://yourwebsite.com/thank-you.html" />

      <!-- Optional: Honeypot field for bot protection (keep hidden) -->
      <input type="text" name="_gotcha" style="display:none !important" tabindex="-1" autocomplete="off" />

      <!-- Submit Button -->
      <button type="submit" class="submit-btn">Send Message</button>
    </form>
  </main>
</body>
</html>`}
/>

## Step-by-step explanation

<div class="mfc-step-item">
  <div class="mfc-step-num">1</div>
  <div class="mfc-step-content">
    <h3 class="mfc-step-title">Set the Form Action URL</h3>
    <p>
      The <code>action</code> attribute points directly to your unique MyFormCapture endpoint.
      Each form in your dashboard receives a dedicated UUID such as <code>https://myformcapture.com/f/your-form-uuid</code>.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mfc-step-item">
  <div class="mfc-step-num">2</div>
  <div class="mfc-step-content">
    <h3 class="mfc-step-title">Specify the POST Method</h3>
    <p>
      Always configure <code>method="POST"</code>. MyFormCapture processes incoming form data over secure HTTPS POST requests.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mfc-step-item">
  <div class="mfc-step-num">3</div>
  <div class="mfc-step-content">
    <h3 class="mfc-step-title">Assign Descriptive Input Names</h3>
    <p>
      MyFormCapture maps submission columns directly from each input's <code>name</code> attribute (e.g. <code>name="name"</code>, <code>name="email"</code>, <code>name="message"</code>). Make sure each field has a unique name.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mfc-step-item">
  <div class="mfc-step-num">4</div>
  <div class="mfc-step-content">
    <h3 class="mfc-step-title">Add Custom Redirect & Spam Prevention</h3>
    <p>
      Adding <code>&lt;input type="hidden" name="_next" value="https://..."&gt;</code> automatically redirects users back to your custom thank-you page. Adding <code>name="_gotcha"</code> acts as a silent honeypot that catches automated spam bots.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
